# HOPE EDU guided demonstration scripts

All records are synthetic. Before presenting, use Demo settings to reset the dataset. The reporting date is fixed at 16 November 2026. Role switching demonstrates responsibilities; it does not represent real user authentication.

## Program Intelligence

Start at the national overview. Explain that readiness derives from validated reporting records, then select the critical school infrastructure evidence gap, DLR-07. Switch to State manager, assign the responsible unit and corrective action, and set a deadline. Submit a result with synthetic evidence. Switch to Evidence verifier and validate. Return to the overview to see the updated readiness count. The eight pillars and 28 DLRs are placeholders awaiting authoritative definitions.

## SchoolOps

Open the failed inverter on the asset registry, AST-001, and its linked work order WO-001. As State manager, dispatch a technician. As Technician, record diagnosis, vendor, and a sample quote. As State manager, approve repair. As Technician, attach synthetic repair evidence and confirm the functional test. As Evidence verifier, verify closure. Open the asset health record to show its operational condition and the new timeline event.

## TeacherOps

Open OBS-001 through Guided demo. As Teacher, record the training reflection and confirm completion. As Mentor, complete the planning and participation rubric, provide feedback, schedule follow-up, and record the follow-up result. Open TCH-001 to show the shared development history. Explain that the displayed learning outcome aggregates are illustrative and do not change automatically when training completes.

## Auroch Infrastructure

Open INS-001. As Auroch manager, record the load, supply availability, electrical risk, and connectivity. Select an illustrative tier and record constraints. Submit installation and commissioning evidence. As Evidence verifier, accept commissioning. Open Monitoring and follow the new asset link into SchoolOps. Report a service issue using an operational role to demonstrate continuity into maintenance.

## Essence Teacher Centre

Explore the Training hub and download a sample lesson guide. Open COH-001. As Essence coordinator, confirm prerequisites and start date. As Mentor, record at least four of five attended sessions, submit the sample practicum rubric and feedback, and confirm requirements. As Essence coordinator, record a follow-up date and plan. Open TCH-001 to show training and practicum events in the same teacher history used by TeacherOps.

## Open Classroom

Open REF-001. As Community facilitator, review the coded referral, select a cohort and support need, and confirm enrollment review. Record at least three attended sessions and material delivery. Submit progress evidence and a proposed transition destination. As Community reviewer, validate the transition. Show that restricted referral views are unavailable to ordinary operational roles; no actual safeguarding cases are seeded.

## Field behavior

Use a narrow browser viewport to review the maintenance, mentoring, and outreach forms. Enable Offline simulation before submitting an action. The record saves locally and a queued-change indicator appears. Disable the simulator and sync. For a conflict demonstration, open the conflict control and retain the local version. This is an explicit simulation, not a live network synchronization service.
